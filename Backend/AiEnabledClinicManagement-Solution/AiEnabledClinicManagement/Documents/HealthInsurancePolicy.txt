Clinical Health Insurance Policy
A deductible is the fixed amount a patient must pay out of pocket for covered healthcare services before the insurance plan begins to pay.
A co-payment is a fixed fee that a patient pays at the time of service for a covered healthcare visit or procedure.
Pre-authorization is the approval required from the insurance provider before certain procedures, tests, or medications are covered.
Patients are responsible for paying the full cost of any non-covered services that are not included in their insurance plan.
Submitting a claim to the insurance provider does not guarantee payment, as claims are subject to review and may be denied.
Patients should verify their insurance coverage and benefits prior to receiving treatment to avoid unexpected costs.
