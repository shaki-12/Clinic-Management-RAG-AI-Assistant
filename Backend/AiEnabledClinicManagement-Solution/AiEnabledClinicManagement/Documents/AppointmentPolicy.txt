Clinical Appointment Policy
Patients should arrive at least 15 minutes before their scheduled appointment time to allow for check-in and registration.
Appointments must be cancelled at least 24 hours in advance to avoid a cancellation fee.
Patients may reschedule an appointment by contacting the clinic at least 24 hours before the scheduled time, subject to availability.
A no-show is defined as a patient who fails to attend a scheduled appointment without cancelling or rescheduling at least 24 hours in advance.
Patients with repeated no-shows may be required to pay a deposit before booking future appointments.
Walk-in patients will be seen based on provider availability and may experience longer wait times than scheduled patients.
